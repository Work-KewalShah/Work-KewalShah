<p>
Network Engineer with hands-on experience securing real-world infrastructure, and two internships at Rashtriya Raksha University building computer vision systems for defence applications. Certified CompTIA Network+ (814/900) and Security+ (789/900).

I learn by building. Not tutorials, not courses - I build things, break them, and figure out why they broke. That's produced a Raspberry Pi 5 pentesting cyberdeck (CIPHER), a complete embedded device shipped in 7 days from scratch (Countdown Timer V1), and a drone surveillance system presented to the Airport Authority of India. If I don't understand something, I build a version of it until I do.

Currently deepening my cybersecurity background through homelab projects, wireless security assessments, and whatever I can take apart next.
